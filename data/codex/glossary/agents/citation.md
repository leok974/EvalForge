# Citation

## Definition
A **citation** is a pointer to the source chunk(s) that support a claim. Citations make grounding auditable and reduce “trust me” answers.

## Tiny example
“Refunds are allowed within 30 days [policy:chunk-1].”

## Common pitfall
Citing the wrong chunk is worse than not citing. A good rule is: every major factual claim must cite at least one chunk, and citations must actually contain the supporting statement.
