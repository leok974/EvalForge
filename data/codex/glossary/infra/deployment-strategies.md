# Deployment Strategies

Methods for releasing new application versions.

## Common Strategies
1. **Rolling**: Gradual replacement
2. **Blue-Green**: Swap environments
3. **Canary**: Small % of traffic first
4. **Recreate**: Full downtime

## Considerations
- Downtime tolerance
- Rollback speed
- Resource requirements
