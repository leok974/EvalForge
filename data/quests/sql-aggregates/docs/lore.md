# Lore: Summarizing the Sum

Professor Null observes as the High Scribe calculates the harvest. "Individual records are like single drops of rain," he says. "But when they aggregate, they become a river. The Power of Summation is our greatest analytical tool. It allows us to step back and see the entire landscape."

He looks at the scrolling lines of code. "Find the total count and the total value. We must know the depth of the Archive’s resources."
