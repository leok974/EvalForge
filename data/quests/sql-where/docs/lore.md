# Lore: The Detroit Resistance

"The digital archives are vast," Professor Null explains, adjusting his spectacles. "To find the signal in the noise, you must learn to ignore the data that doesn't matter. In Detroit, the resistance is quiet, but they are there. Find them, and you find the key to the next sector."

He taps the screen, highlighting a flickering line of [SQL](glossary/sql/select). "Precision is your greatest weapon. Filter wisely."
