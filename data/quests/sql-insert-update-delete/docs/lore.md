# Lore: The Living Archive

Professor Null walks along a wall of flowing data. "The Archive is not a tomb," he says, his voice echoeing in the vast chamber. "It is a breathing, shifting thing. It grows as we add records, it corrects itself as truths are refined, and it sheds what is no longer needed."

He stops at a terminal. "Be the architect of this shift. Add the new, correct the old, and purge the obsolete. Only through mutation can the Archive remain vital."
