# Lore: The Wisdom of Crowds

Professor Null gestures toward a cluster of glowing nodes. "Individual data points are like solitary stars," he whispers. "But when they group together, they form constellations. Their collective pattern reveals truths that a single record could never explain."

He taps the terminal. "Group these products. See if their collective value merits our continued support of their sector. Wisdom lies in the group, not the individual."
