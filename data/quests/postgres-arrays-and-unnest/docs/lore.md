"A relational database," Kai said softly, "is not a spreadsheet."

He zoomed the holodisplay in on the `employees` table. The `skills` column pulsated lightly, packed with dense brackets like `{Python, SQL, Go}`.

"Some systems force you to build endless associative tables just to store a list," Kai explained. "PostgreSQL is more evolved. It treats arrays as first-class citizens. You can query them, index them, and expand them directly."

He swept a hand, and the arrays burst open, flattening into a long, beautifully normalized column of individual skills. "We call this unnesting. Unlocking the matrix within."
