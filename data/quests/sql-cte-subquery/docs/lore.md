# Lore: The Recursive Mind

"Complexity is a labyrinth," Professor Null muses, his gaze lost in the scrolling patterns of the Archive's core. "If you try to navigate the whole at once, you will surely lose your way. But if you name the turns, if you label the corridors, the path becomes clear."

He gestures to the terminal. "Create a named expression for our active users. Build the foundation before you attempt the summation. In the recursive mind, clarity is the only map."
