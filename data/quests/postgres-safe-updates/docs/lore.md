"To build is human," Kai said, leaning against the server rack. "To destroy is also human. But to destroy everything at once? That requires an `UPDATE` without a `WHERE` clause."

He pulled up a holographic historical record from the Archives. It showed a massive red spike in system downtime from the Tech Bubble era.

"A junior engineer wanted to reset their own password in the production database. They typed `UPDATE users SET password = 'password123';` and hit enter. They didn't specify which user." 

Kai snapped his fingers.

"Eighty million users suddenly had the exact same password. It took three days to restore the backup. Always use your `WHERE` clause. Treat it like the safety on a plasma cutter."
