# Lore: The Empty Spaces

"An archivist's mind often seeks the filled shelves," Professor Null remarks, pacing the quiet halls of the Zero Sector. "But the empty spaces speak just as loudly. They tell of potential unrealized, of silence where there should be song."

He stops at a flickering terminal. "Find the users who have never placed an order. They are the empty spaces in our history. Perhaps, by findidng them, we can understand why they remained silent."
