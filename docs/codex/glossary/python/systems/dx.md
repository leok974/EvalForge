# Developer Experience (DX)

Developer Experience is the technical equivalent of User Experience (UX), focusing on the friction, speed, and sentiment of engineers interacting with a codebase or platform.

### Why DX Matters in Systems
In large Python systems, poor DX leads to "technical debt" and slow delivery. High-quality tooling, clear documentation, and fast feedback loops are core pillars of DX.

### Key DX Improvements
1.  **Fast Feedback**: Instant linting and rapid unit tests.
2.  **Discoverability**: Clear service boundaries and searchable documentation.
3.  **Automation**: Scripting repetitive tasks to remove "toil".

### Relevance in EvalForge
Quests like **Internal Tooling & DX** teach you to value the time of your fellow engineers by building the infrastructure they rely on every day.
